      About this app

      E-Bill Estimator turns the units on your electricity meter into an estimated bill, using a four-block tariff and an optional rebate of 0–5%.

      How to use it

      1. Calculate. Select a month, enter your units (kWh) and choose a rebate, then press Calculate & save.
      2. View history. Open the History tab to see every saved bill, with a chart of the latest ones.
      3. Edit or delete. Tap any record to see full details, then edit or delete it.
